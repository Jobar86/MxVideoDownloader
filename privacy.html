Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Mx Video Downloader ("the app") lets you download videos and audio from links you provide, for your own personal use. This policy explains what data is involved when you use the app.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier. There is no server-side account system — your download history and settings are stored only on your own device.

2. Advertising

The app is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show ads (including personalized ads where permitted), Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as your advertising ID, device information, IP address, and general location, and may use cookies or similar technology. This collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy. Where required, you will be shown a consent form on first launch to control ad personalization, in line with applicable regulations (e.g. GDPR in the EU/EEA).

3. Permissions used by the app

  • Internet: required to fetch video information and perform downloads.
  • Notifications: used to show download progress and completion status.
  • Foreground service: used to keep downloads running reliably while the app is in the background.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit personal data to us.

4. Downloaded files

Files you download are saved directly to your device's Movies or Music folders using Android's standard media storage. We do not have access to, and do not upload, the files you download.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that any content you download using this app is used in accordance with applicable copyright law and the terms of service of the platform the content came from.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above and made available in this same page and in the app.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at jo.barr86@gmail.com.
